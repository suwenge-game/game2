Category notes

Browser shooters divide cleanly into three families: top-down arena games where mouse aim rules, first-person portal games where movement rules, and tactical shooters where cover rules. The reviews below identify the family first, because the family predicts the experience far better than the theme. Every game links to its partner host with the outbound link clearly marked; our role is curation and context, not hosting.

Bloxd io — 本作鼓励玩家在实践中掌握听声辨位与团队协同，稳步记住补给位置比盲目冒险有效。 Full review: Bloxd io guide.

Masked Special Forces — Masked Special Forces（射击）强调掩体利用与准星控制，先求稳定再提升难度。 Full review: Masked Special Forces guide.

GTA Simulator — 围绕本作制定训练计划时，应把掩体利用与准星控制和利用掩体试探列为优先事项。 Plays on its partner host: open GTA Simulator externally.

Fragen — 本作的乐趣来自逐渐看懂武器选择和移动射击，并用先校准瞄准灵敏度突破下一阶段。 Plays on its partner host: open Fragen externally.

DTA 6 — 本作把地图观察与交火距离放在核心位置，先保持移动与观察再尝试连续遭遇战会更稳妥。 Plays on its partner host: open DTA 6 externally.

Stickman GTA City — Stickman GTA City（射击）强调掩体利用与准星控制，先求稳定再提升难度。 Plays on its partner host: open Stickman GTA City externally.

FPS Simulator — 进入本作后别急着追求速度，记住补给位置才是应对近距离交火的可靠起点。 Plays on its partner host: open FPS Simulator externally.

Crazy Strike Force — Crazy Strike Force（射击）强调掩体利用与准星控制，先求稳定再提升难度。 Plays on its partner host: open Crazy Strike Force externally.

Shell Shockers.io — Shell Shockers.io（射击）强调掩体利用与准星控制，先求稳定再提升难度。 Plays on its partner host: open Shell Shockers.io externally.

Deadshot io — 从新手角度看，本作最值得先练的是武器选择和移动射击，随后再先校准瞄准灵敏度。 Plays on its partner host: open Deadshot io externally.

Mob City — 本作适合用短回合反复练习，围绕地图观察与交火距离并保持移动与观察即可持续进步。 Plays on its partner host: open Mob City externally.

Crazy Shooters — 游玩本作时应关注弹药管理和目标优先级，尤其要避免暴露侧身，才能从容处理关键据点。 Plays on its partner host: open Crazy Shooters externally.

How we review

Every entry in this portal was played long enough to answer three questions: what the core verb is, how difficulty scales, and what a complete session looks like. We do not score games with numeric stars — numeric scores invite false precision and invite padding. Instead each review states the game's family, its session target, and the player it suits. Outbound play links are marked as partner links: the games are hosted by their original developers, and our value is the reading we add, not the hosting we pretend to do.

Update cadence: this roster reflects the catalog as of September 2026. When a partner host retires a game, its entry is marked and later pruned rather than silently rotting — broken links are a review failure. Corrections and disputes go through the contact channel.
